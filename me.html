M E

M E is a narrative game in which you play as a teenager trapped in their bedroom. The game takes place in said bedroom, where the player must complete chores in the form of minigames. During the minigames strange and distressing events take place, reflecting the main character's loss of sanity.

This game has been developed by 2 people in a weekend (from Friday to Sunday) for the SCREAM JAM 2025.
