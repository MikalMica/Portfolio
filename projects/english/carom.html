Carom

Carom is a roguelike game in which the player move across different pool tables playing billiards. It's possible to play up to 5 rounds until you have to fight a final boss who will make the game progress towards the next table after being defeated. Each boss has unique mechanics that sets them apart from regular rounds.
Each completed round rewards the player, and each non-completed round gives the player a billiard ball with effects that can be used to their advantage on other rounds.

This game has been developed by 9 people in four months as a university project.
I did:

  • Part of the UI design
