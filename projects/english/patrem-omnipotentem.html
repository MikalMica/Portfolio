Patrem Omnipotentem

Patrem Omnipotentem is a narrative game which takes place in a distopian future ruled by a super-computer which is considered a saviour and a deity. The player explores different places in this world, interacting with its people, who will reveal information about the super-computer that rules the world and the context of said world.
Some of these characters will start battles in which the player must avoid their attacks similarly to games like Undertale.

This game has been developed by 6 people in a weekend (from Friday to Sunday) for the ComJamOn 2026 Powered by EA and won the best ambience mention.
